Income Tax Basics

What is Income Tax?

Income tax is a direct tax levied by the government on the income earned by individuals and entities during a financial year.

Who Needs to Pay Income Tax?

  • Individuals: Based on income slabs and age
  • HUFs: Hindu Undivided Families
  • Companies: Domestic and foreign companies
  • Firms: Partnership firms and LLPs

Types of Income

  • Salary Income: Earnings from employment
  • Business/Professional Income: Profits from business
  • Capital Gains: Profit from asset sales
  • House Property Income: Rental income
  • Other Sources: Interest, dividends, etc.

Important Dates

  • Financial Year: April 1 to March 31
  • Assessment Year: Year following the Financial Year
  • Due Date: July 31 (for individuals)
