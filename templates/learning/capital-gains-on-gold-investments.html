Capital Gains on Gold Investments

Gold can be held as physical gold, ETFs, or digital gold.

  • LTCG (Held for 3+ years): Taxed at 20% with indexation.
  • STCG (Held <3 years): Taxed as per individual slab.

How to Reduce Tax on Gold?

  • Invest in Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) for tax-free LTCG after 8 years.
  • Use Gold ETFs instead of physical gold to save on GST & making charges.
