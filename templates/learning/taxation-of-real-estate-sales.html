Taxation of Real Estate Sales

Short-Term (Held for less than 2 years): Gains are taxed as per individual slab rate.

Long-Term (Held for 2+ years): Taxed at 20% with indexation benefits.

How to Reduce Tax on Real Estate Gains?

  • Section 54: Reinvest in another residential property to claim an exemption.
  • Capital Gain Bonds (Section 54EC): Invest up to ₹50 lakh in bonds (NHAI, REC) to get tax exemption.
