Tax-Free Investments

Some investments offer completely tax-free returns, making them attractive for long-term savings:

  • Public Provident Fund (PPF): Interest is tax-free under Section 80C.
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF): Maturity proceeds are tax-free if held for 5+ years.
  • Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs): LTCG is tax-free if held till maturity (8 years).
  • Life Insurance Maturity: Payouts from ULIPs & Endowment Plans are tax-free under Section 10(10D).
