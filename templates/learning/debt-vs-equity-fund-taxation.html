Debt vs Equity Fund Taxation

Investment Type Short-Term Gains (STCG) Long-Term Gains (LTCG)
Equity Funds 15% (if held <1 year) 10% (if held >1 year, after ₹1 lakh exemption)
Debt Funds Taxed as per income slab (if held <3 years) 20% with indexation (if held >3 years)
Hybrid Funds (Equity >65%) Same as equity taxation Same as equity taxation
Hybrid Funds (Equity <65%) Taxed as per slab 20% with indexation
