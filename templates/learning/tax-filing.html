Tax Filing Process

Steps to File Income Tax Returns

  1. Gather Documents:
    • Form 16 from employer
    • Bank statements
    • Investment proofs
    • Property documents
  2. Choose Correct ITR Form:
    • ITR-1 for salaried individuals
    • ITR-2 for capital gains
    • ITR-3 for business income
  3. File Returns:
    • Online through Income Tax portal
    • Through tax filing websites
    • Through a tax professional

Important Deadlines

  • Regular Filing: July 31
  • Audit Cases: October 31
  • Revised Return: December 31
