Long-Term vs Short-Term Capital Gains

What are Capital Gains?

Capital gains arise when you sell an asset at a price higher than its purchase price. These gains are classified as:

  • Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG): If an asset is held for less than the specified duration before selling.
  • Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG): If an asset is held for longer than the specified period before selling.
Asset Type Holding Period for LTCG Tax Rate
Equity Shares & Mutual Funds More than 1 year 10% (after ₹1 lakh exemption)
Real Estate More than 2 years 20% with Indexation
Debt Mutual Funds More than 3 years 20% with Indexation
Gold & Other Assets More than 3 years 20% with Indexation

Short-term gains are taxed at 15% (for equity) and as per slab (for other assets).
