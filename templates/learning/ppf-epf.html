PPF and EPF Tax Benefits

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

  • Investment limit: ₹1.5 lakh per year
  • Interest rate: ~7.1% (reviewed quarterly)
  • Lock-in period: 15 years
  • EEE tax status (Exempt at all stages)

Employee Provident Fund (EPF)

  • 12% of Basic + DA from employee
  • Matching contribution from employer
  • Interest rate: ~8.15% (2023-24)
  • Tax-free interest up to ₹2.5 lakh per year
