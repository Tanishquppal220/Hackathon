Indexation Benefit in LTCG

Indexation adjusts the purchase price of assets to account for inflation, reducing taxable gains.

Formula:

\[ \text{Indexed Cost} = \text{Original Cost} \times \frac{\text{CII of Sale Year}}{\text{CII of Purchase Year}} \]

Where CII is the Cost Inflation Index.

Example:

  • Bought property for ₹10 lakh in 2005 (CII = 117)
  • Sold in 2023 (CII = 331)
  • Indexed cost = (10,00,000 × 331) / 117 = ₹28,29,000
  • If sold for ₹40 lakh, taxable LTCG = ₹11.71 lakh instead of ₹30 lakh.
