Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)

Key Features

  • Shortest lock-in period among Section 80C options (3 years)
  • Potential for higher returns through equity investment
  • Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80C
  • LTCG tax of 10% on gains above ₹1 lakh

Advantages

  • Higher return potential
  • Lower lock-in period
  • Professional management
  • Start with small amounts (SIP)

Considerations

  • Market-linked returns
  • Risk of capital loss
  • Need to track performance
  • Exit load may apply
