Indian Tax Laws

India's tax system is governed by the Income Tax Act of 1961. Key provisions include the classification of income into five heads: Salaries, Income from House Property, Profits and Gains of Business or Profession, Capital Gains, and Income from Other Sources. The Act mandates that individuals earning above a certain threshold must file income tax returns annually. Deductions under Section 80C, such as investments in PPF, NSC, and life insurance premiums, can reduce taxable income. Additionally, the Goods and Services Tax (GST) implemented in 2017, replaced multiple indirect taxes with a unified tax structure, simplifying compliance for businesses. Understanding these laws is crucial for effective tax planning and compliance.
